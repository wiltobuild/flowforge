## Who this is for

People who already use Claude Pro daily and have genuinely run out of room — not people
guessing they might need more. Max exists to remove a real, repeated bottleneck, not to
buy peace of mind in advance.

## What you get

<PlanFacts planId="claude-max" />

## What it's good at

Heavy, sustained agentic work: long Claude Code sessions, multiple parallel subagents,
back-to-back tasks in one sitting that would burn through Pro's window well before the
day is over.

## What it can't do

It doesn't change the *mechanism* — you still hit a wall at the session boundary, just a
much bigger one. If your workload genuinely needs more than 20x Pro's usage in a single
window, Max won't solve that either.

## How the limits feel

Identical mechanism to Pro, just scaled: a hard stop at the session boundary unless usage
credits are enabled, in which case you can pay your way through at standard API rates.
The multiplier (5x or 20x Pro) determines how much work fits before you'd ever see that
wall in practice.

## Who should pick something else

If you haven't actually hit Pro's limit with real, regular use, [Claude
Pro](../claude-pro/) is the better default — the price jump only pays
for itself once the limit is a recurring problem, not a hypothetical one.

## Verify

Pricing here is listed as a starting point ("from") rather than one fixed number — the
5x and 20x tiers aren't broken out separately on this page yet. Check Claude's own pricing
page directly before committing to a specific tier.
