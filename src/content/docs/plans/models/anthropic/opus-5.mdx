## Best at

Complex agentic coding and enterprise workloads — Anthropic's own recommendation is to
start here if you're unsure which model to use for serious build work.

## Weak at

Costs more than Sonnet for routine, non-complex tasks, where the extra capability buys
you little.

## Quota weight

Heavy. Treat it the way you'd treat Fable 5 — a deliberate choice for a hard step, not the
default you leave running for everything.

## When to pick it

Complex agentic coding, architecture-level decisions, or enterprise-grade work where
reliability matters more than speed or cost.

## When not to

Everyday coding, quick edits, or anything Sonnet already handles comfortably — which is
most day-to-day work.

## Notes and gotchas

None specific beyond the general heavy-model guidance above: mix models within a session
rather than running everything on Opus 5 by default.
