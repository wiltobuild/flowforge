## Who this is for

Someone trying ChatGPT for the first time with no intention of paying yet.

## What you get

<PlanFacts planId="chatgpt-free" />

## What it's good at

Light, occasional use — a question, a quick draft, getting a feel for the product.

## What it can't do

Not built for regular coding or agentic work. No Codex access at this tier.

## How the limits feel

Reported to degrade gracefully under load — you're more likely to get bumped to a lighter
model than to be hard-blocked — but this site could not confirm the exact mechanism
against an official OpenAI page (see Verify).

## Who should pick something else

Anyone doing regular work should look at [ChatGPT
Plus](../chatgpt-plus/) instead, which is the entry paid tier most people
actually use daily.

check this site has attempted (most recently 2026-08-23). If you're deciding based on
