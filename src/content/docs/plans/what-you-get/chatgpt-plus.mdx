## Who this is for

Individual builders who want Codex alongside general ChatGPT chat use, at the same entry
price point as Claude Pro (see the price above).

## What you get

<PlanFacts planId="chatgpt-plus" />

## What it's good at

Reported to be a genuinely good-value tier that's held its price for an extended period
while gaining features — Codex, Deep Research, and Sora among them.

## What it can't do

Codex on Plus is reported to run on a message-band system rather than a flat count, and
OpenAI has reportedly changed how the 5-hour cap works more than once recently (including
removing it entirely as a "temporary" change with no stated end date). This site could
not confirm whether that change is still in effect as of the date above.

## How the limits feel

Reported as message-band limits rather than a hard token count, with Codex specifically
said to hard-stop at a weekly ceiling once exhausted. Not independently confirmed — see
Verify.

## Who should pick something else

If Plus's Codex limits have become a repeated bottleneck, [ChatGPT
Pro](../chatgpt-pro/) is the next tier up. If you're not sure you'll use
Codex regularly, try [ChatGPT Free](../chatgpt-free/) first.

## Verify

**This entire page is community-reported.** `chatgpt.com/pricing` and OpenAI's own
Codex-limits help article returned an access error on two separate checks 8 days apart
(most recently 2026-08-23). Confirm current pricing and limits directly on OpenAI's site
before deciding based on anything here.
