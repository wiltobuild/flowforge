## Who this is for

Most individual builders doing daily work with Claude Code on a single, predictable
monthly cost. This is the plan most people in this cohort should default to unless
they've already confirmed they need more.

## What you get

<PlanFacts planId="claude-pro" />

## What it's good at

Real daily agentic coding. Chat and Claude Code draw from the same usage pool, so a
morning of chatting about a design and an afternoon of Claude Code implementing it both
count against the same window — which is usually fine, because most days don't fill it.

## What it can't do

It won't quietly absorb a heavy multi-agent run or a long unattended session without
hitting the wall — Pro's window is generous for normal use, not unlimited for anything you
throw at it. And unless you've opted in, Anthropic won't let you pay your way past a full
session; you wait.

## How the limits feel

This is the plan's most important fact, and it's not what most people assume. If you
haven't turned on usage credits, hitting the 5-hour session limit is a hard stop — the
tool simply won't respond until the window resets. If you *have* turned on usage credits,
you can keep going, billed at standard API rates on the card you have on file. Decide
which of those two experiences you want before you need it, not in the middle of a task.

## Who should pick something else

If you're regularly hitting the session wall even with a normal day's work, [Claude
Max](../claude-max/) gives you 5x or 20x the room at a higher price. If
you're not sure you'll use Claude daily yet, start with [Claude
Free](../claude-free/) instead.

## Verify

The facts above — price, what's included, and exactly how the limit behaves — are pulled
directly from Anthropic's own pages and dated. Anthropic has changed weekly-limit
promotions more than once recently; if the staleness note above is showing a warning,
don't treat this page as current.
