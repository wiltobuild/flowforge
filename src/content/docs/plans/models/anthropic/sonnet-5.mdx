<ModelFacts modelId="claude-sonnet-5" />

## Best at

The best combination of speed and intelligence for most production agentic work. If
you're not sure which model to reach for, this is the reasonable default.

## Weak at

Less capable than Opus or Fable 5 on the hardest reasoning tasks — it won't be the right
choice for the rare problem that genuinely needs the top of the lineup.

## Quota weight

Moderate. Comfortable to run repeatedly through a normal working session without it
becoming the thing that exhausts your plan.

## When to pick it

Everyday agentic coding, drafting, and analysis — the majority of day-to-day work.

## When not to

A task you already know is unusually hard or high-stakes, where the extra reliability of
Opus 5 or Fable 5 is worth the added cost and latency.

## Notes and gotchas

None specific. This is the model most people should be running most of the time.

None currently linked. This site doesn't publish benchmark scores — see this site's
product brief for why.
